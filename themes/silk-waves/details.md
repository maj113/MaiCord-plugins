A theme that changes with the time of day and the month, the way the Silk Waves sky does. At night the surfaces are deep and near-black, lit by the month's accent; by day they turn light, softly tinted by the month's day sky. Dawn and dusk sit between: a lifted, sky-tinted dark at dawn and a deeper-tinted light at dusk.

It turns light and dark on its own: dark from about 21:00 to 09:00, light from 09:00 to 21:00, gliding over a second and a half when it changes (at once with reduced motion). Through the hours between two phases of the same base, its colours move a little each minute.

## The day

The theme follows the Silk Waves effect's own schedule: night from 23:00 to 01:00, dawn from 05:00 to 07:00, day from 11:00 to 13:00 and dusk from 17:00 to 19:00, each blending into the next over the hours between. Across a change between light and dark, the nearer phase shows whole, so the text never passes through grey; the change happens halfway, at 09:00 and 21:00.

## The month

Each month brings its own colours, taken from Silk Waves' palette: the night glow gives the night and dawn their accent and tint, the day sky the day's surfaces and accent, and both together the dusk. As in the sky, a month's colours hold until the 5th and then turn slowly toward the next month's, reaching them at the month's end.

## Background effect

It asks for the Silk Waves effect, which MaiCord applies when Silk Waves is installed (installing the theme offers it). While this theme is on, the effect's day and night follow the theme rather than Windows: the light theme by day shows Silk Waves' day sky, and the night the night sky. Without the effect the theme is flat.

## Readability

Text reaches at least 7.9:1 and muted text at least 4.9:1 on every surface, in every phase, month and step between, and MaiCord reports no contrast warnings for it at any time of day.

## Older MaiCord

It needs MaiCord 0.6.4 or later; earlier versions don't list it. A MaiCord before 0.6.4 that imports the file by hand shows its night look, in October's colours, all day.
